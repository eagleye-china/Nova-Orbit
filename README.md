## 🤔 Why Nova-Orbit?

Web-based AI apps (like Gradio or Streamlit) are slow and clunky. **Nova-Orbit** brings AI directly to your desktop with zero compromise:

- 🌱 **"Growing" Streaming**: Bubbles dynamically resize as text generates. Smooth, jank-free scrolling powered by QML.
- 🟩 **Integrated System Logs**: Neon green terminal-style logs live natively alongside chat bubbles. Perfect for Agentic workflows.
- 🖱️ **Native Desktop Feel**: Drag & drop files, native shortcuts, and pure dark mode aesthetics.
